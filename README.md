## 🚀 About Me

Hi there! I'm **Anas El Ouarzadi** – a Computer Systems Engineer specializing in **Artificial Intelligence** 🤖, **Data Science** 📊, and **Embedded Systems** ⚙️, currently pursuing a Master's degree in Intelligent Systems for Energy and Transport at the **University of Málaga**.

I work at the intersection of AI and hardware — training machine learning models, then deploying them on real edge devices like Raspberry Pi. My focus spans:

- 🧠 Machine Learning & Deep Learning: computer vision, GANs, GPU/TPU-accelerated training
- 🔒 AI for Cybersecurity: intrusion detection systems for IoT networks
- ⚙️ Embedded Systems: microcontrollers, CAN bus, industrial automation (Grafcet/Ladder Logic)
- 🌍 Multilingual & mobile: based in Spain, fluent in Arabic, English, French, and Spanish

Always building end-to-end — from data collection to a working, deployed system. Available for freelance projects in AI model development and embedded/IoT solutions. ✨
